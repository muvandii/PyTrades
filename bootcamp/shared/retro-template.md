# Phase <N> retro: <phase name>

> End of every phase. Twenty minutes, three questions, no hedging. Save as
> `reports/retro-<phase>.md` - the Week 9 gate checks this file exists and answers
> at least two of the three questions by name.

## 1. What pattern killed the most strategies?

Look at the graveyard, count causes, name the top one and the count.
(e.g. "cost death: 4 of 7 graves, every one with turnover above 40%/month")

## 2. Which concept made the biggest difference?

The one you would teach first to past-you, with the number that proves it
(e.g. "shifting the signal: OOS Sharpe went from 1.6 fake to 0.4 real").

## 3. What am I still fooling myself about?

The place where your evidence is weakest and your confidence is highest. Be specific:
"my momentum result rests on 2010-2021", "my pairs trade has 14 trades", "I have never
tested my sizing rule on a losing streak longer than 3".
