# D05 — Backtester v1

## Objective

Replace `kit/spine.py` with your own event-driven backtester that consumes the same four contracts, executes signals one bar late, tracks cash and positions explicitly, and reproduces buy-and-hold within 1%.

## Teaches

A backtest is an accounting system that happens to touch markets. Until positions, cash and turnover are explicit quantities in your code, you are doing arithmetic on a chart, not simulating a portfolio.

## Build

**Module layout (`engine/`):**

```
engine/
├── events.py       # the event/accounting loop: bars -> targets -> fills -> positions -> equity
├── backtest.py     # the public surface: run_backtest + BacktestResult
├── metrics.py      # Week 3: vol, sharpe, sortino (extended in Week 4)
├── costs.py        # Week 3: turnover-based cost model
├── data.py         # loading prices through the DATA contract
├── tearsheet.py    # Week 4
├── walkforward.py  # Week 4
└── README.md       # what exists, what changed, what it broke
```

**Required behaviours** (full list in [spec/engine-spec.md](../spec/engine-spec.md)):

1. `run_backtest(spec, name=None, prices=None, signals=None, start=None, end=None, initial_capital=100_000, fee_bps=0.0, slippage_bps=0.0, execution="next_open", data_dir="data", signal_shift=1)`.
2. Signal at bar `t` becomes a position at bar `t+1`. The shift happens once, in one place.
3. Positions and cash tracked explicitly; equity = cash + Σ(position × price).
4. `state_at(date)` returns positions and equity as of that bar.
5. Results saved to `reports/backtests/<name>/` with `equity.csv`, `positions.csv`, `trades.csv`, `metrics.json`, `meta.json`; `BacktestResult.load(name)` round-trips.
6. `meta.json` carries `spec_fingerprint` and `data_fingerprint`.

**Proof obligations:**

- buy-and-hold (constant weight 1.0, `execution="next_close"`) matches the price ratio within 1%;
- the time-travel test: truncating the data at T leaves positions up to T unchanged;
- `make test-contract E=engine.backtest` passes.

## Definition of done

- [ ] `engine/backtest.py` implements the documented signature and behaviours
- [ ] `make test-contract E=engine.backtest` passes all contract tests
- [ ] Buy-and-hold reproduction within 1% is demonstrated and saved
- [ ] The time-travel test is implemented and passing
- [ ] One strategy run through your engine and through `kit.spine` agrees to within rounding
- [ ] `engine/README.md` documents the loop, the data structures and the known limits
