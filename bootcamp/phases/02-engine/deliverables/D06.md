# D06 — Cost model + cost sensitivity report

## Objective

Implement a turnover-based cost model inside the engine and publish a cost-sensitivity report that names the breakeven cost of at least one strategy.

## Teaches

Costs are a function of turnover, not of sincerity. What looks like an edge at 0 bps is often a liquidity donation: the strategy pays the spread provider for the privilege of trading.

## Build

1. `engine/costs.py`:

   ```python
   def bar_cost(weight_delta, fee_bps, slippage_bps):
       """Cost charged on the notional traded this bar, as a fraction of equity."""
       return abs(weight_delta).sum() * (fee_bps + slippage_bps) / 10_000
   ```

   Charged inside the loop. If you find yourself subtracting costs in the reporting layer, stop: the engine is lying to you at that point.

2. Run one strategy (your best from Phase 1, or `S01_ma_cross` from the course templates) across a bps grid: 0, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 10, 15, 20, 30.
3. Write `reports/cost-sensitivity.md`:

   | fee (bps/side) | CAGR | Sharpe | max DD | total return |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

   plus:
   - **breakeven cost** (the bps where CAGR crosses zero, interpolated),
   - annual turnover and the implied annual cost at your realistic fee assumption,
   - one paragraph: for this strategy, is the edge in the signal or in the accounting?

4. Sensitivity to execution, not just fees: re-run at `execution="next_open"` and `execution="next_close"`. The gap between them is the structural cost of not being able to trade the close you decided on.

## Definition of done

- [ ] `engine/costs.py` exists, is imported by the loop, and has a unit test with a hand-computed expected cost
- [ ] `reports/cost-sensitivity.md` contains the full bps grid, the breakeven, and the turnover arithmetic
- [ ] The open-vs-close execution difference is measured and explained
- [ ] A strategy that dies between 5 and 20 bps is documented (or you document that yours survives 30 bps and why)

## Evidence

`engine/costs.py` · `reports/cost-sensitivity.md` · the saved backtest artifacts for each bps level
