# D15 — Risk policy: sizing, allocation, survival

## Objective

Convert signals into a portfolio that can be lived with: explicit sizing rules, an allocation that respects correlation, a measured probability of ruin under your own rules, and a regime contingency - all written down in one page.

## Teaches

Signals decide what to trade; sizing decides whether you survive to trade it. Portfolio construction is where good strategies are most often destroyed, quietly, by rules nobody wrote down.

## Build

1. **`reports/risk-policy.md`** (one page): mandate, position sizing rule + parameters + cap, portfolio construction and rebalance frequency, correlation policy with thresholds and actions, de-risking trigger/action/resume condition, ruin definition.
2. **`reports/portfolio-allocation.md`**: the correlation matrix on the common overlap period, the combination result (Sharpe, vol, max DD) vs equal-weight, the weights you chose and why, and the rebalance cost estimate.
3. **`reports/monte-carlo.md`**: block bootstrap of the *sized* portfolio (2,000 paths), with your sizing rules applied inside each path. Report: median, 5th percentile, P(50% drawdown in a year), P(ruin threshold).
4. **`reports/regime-lab.md`**: regimes defined by an observable, causal rule; per-strategy behaviour per regime; whether a filter helps OOS; the number of regime switches.
5. **Sizing sensitivity grid**: `reports/sensitivity/sizing-*.csv` (3+ files) sweeping target vol, correlation-scaling threshold, and de-risking trigger.

## Required numbers (each one goes in the policy, not just the report)

- [ ] Target portfolio volatility and how it was chosen
- [ ] Max gross exposure and the rule that enforces it
- [ ] Correlation threshold at which gross is reduced, and by how much
- [ ] De-risking trigger, action, and resume condition - both observable
- [ ] P(50% drawdown within a year) under your own rules
- [ ] Expected annual cost drag from rebalancing at the chosen frequency

## Definition of done

- [ ] Four reports exist and cross-reference each other
- [ ] The risk policy fits on one page and contains no un-quantified rule
- [ ] The sized portfolio's Sharpe beats equal-weight on the same signals (or the failure is documented)
- [ ] Every rule in the policy is exercised by the Monte Carlo (rules that are not in the simulation are decoration)
- [ ] Sizing sensitivity grid saved with at least 3 CSVs
- [ ] Graveyard entries for the sizing rules that died
