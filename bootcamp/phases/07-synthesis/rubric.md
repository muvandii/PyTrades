# Phase 7 rubric — Synthesis / capstone (Week 13)

| Criterion | 0 (not yet) | 1 (passing) | 2 (strong) | If you scored low |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Stranger-clone test** | not attempted | passed on a fresh clone in ≤15 min | a stranger (or timer-you) reproduced the headline number, and the fixes found are documented | run the quickstart from a fresh clone and write down every real step |
| **Writeup** | missing | question, method, artifacts, deaths, limitation, next steps | a hostile reader finds no unsupported claim; the limitation is stated by you first | add the artifact link to every number you quote |
| **Portfolio doc** | aspirational list | lines with weights, rules, and evidence links | removal criteria per line; live status; sizing consistent with the Week 10 policy | cut lines without verdicts |
| **Graveyard taxonomy** | a list | 3-5 ranked causes with counts | each cause has a predictive signal you could check before testing an idea | count, rank, and write the leading indicator per cause |
| **Recall test** | skipped | 3 concepts explained, 1 coded from scratch | the failure (if any) was diagnosed and re-done on the spot | do it now; it takes an hour and it is the whole point |
| **Public reach** | local only | repo public, writeup published with a link | a stranger has actually read it and asked a question | publish, then share it with one person |
| **Journal close** | missing | 80+ entries overall | the final entry names the one belief you cannot test yet | write it; it seeds the next quarter |
| **Roadmap** | absent | three experiments | each with a hypothesis, a kill criterion, and a target artifact | write the kill criterion first - if you cannot, it is not an experiment |

## Capstone retro

The final retro is the writeup's "honest limitation" plus the last journal entry. If you want the
three-question form one more time: the pattern that killed most of your strategies (the ranked
graveyard top line), the most valuable concept of the thirteen weeks (usually the one that changed
a sizing or testing habit, not the most mathematically glamorous), and what you are still fooling
yourself about (the honest limitation, restated as a sentence about you rather than about markets).

## Pass bar

- M6 passed: stranger-clone test + recall test documented
- 15+ verdicts, 80+ journal entries, graveyard with a ranked taxonomy
- `WRITEUP.md` contains a public link and no unsupported number
- The repo contains no secrets, keys, or personal notes
- You can state, without notes, the course's success metric in your own numbers: can you debunk a viral strategy in 30 minutes, and take an idea end-to-end in under an hour?
