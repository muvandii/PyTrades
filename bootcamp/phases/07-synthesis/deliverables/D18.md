# D18 — Capstone: public repo + writeup + portfolio + roadmap

## Objective

Ship the whole thing in public: a repo a stranger can clone and run, a writeup that explains what you found, a portfolio document that states what you would trade and why, and a graveyard closed with a taxonomy.

## Teaches

Research you cannot explain to a stranger is research you do not understand. The capstone is not a summary of thirteen weeks - it is the compression of them into artifacts that survive contact with a hostile reader.

## Build

1. **`README.md`** (repo root, for a stranger with 15 minutes):
   - two-sentence description; the 4-command quickstart; artifact map; data source + limitations; link to `WRITEUP.md`; the honest limitation in one line.
   - **Stranger-clone test documented**: date, fresh clone, elapsed time, result.
2. **`WRITEUP.md`** from [capstone/writeup-template.md](../../../capstone/writeup-template.md): question → method → three best artifacts → three instructive deaths → honest limitation → next experiments and a **public link**.
3. **`PORTFOLIO.md`** from [capstone/portfolio-template.md](../../../capstone/portfolio-template.md): lines, weights, sizing/risk rules in one paragraph, evidence per line, removal criteria, live window status.
4. **`graveyard.md`** closed with the taxonomy: 3-5 ranked causes, counts and shares, predictive signal per cause, entries retained under each.
5. **Roadmap**: three experiments with hypotheses and kill criteria.

## Definition of done

- [ ] `README.md`, `WRITEUP.md`, `PORTFOLIO.md` exist; `WRITEUP.md` contains a public link (`http...`)
- [ ] `graveyard.md` has 5+ `##` sections (a section per cause) with ranked counts
- [ ] 15+ strategy verdicts exist under `strategies/*/verdict.md`
- [ ] 80+ journal entries across the course
- [ ] Stranger-clone test performed on a fresh clone and documented
- [ ] Recall test done: three concepts explained from memory, one coded from scratch in an empty file
- [ ] The repo is public and contains no secrets, keys, or personal notes

## Evidence

`README.md` · `WRITEUP.md` · `PORTFOLIO.md` · `graveyard.md`
