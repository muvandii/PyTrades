# D13 — Replication R2: Pairs Trading

## Objective

Rebuild the Gatev-Goetzmann-Rouwenhorst pairs result with a real test for the relationship (cointegration, half-life) and with pair selection performed on training data only.

## Teaches

Cointegration is a relationship between two series, not a property of either one — and selection on the full sample is a look-ahead machine that produces beautiful, unusable results.

## Build

1. **`papers/R2_pairs/impl.py`**
   - formation window: hedge ratio by OLS on log prices, spread = `log(A) − β·log(B)`;
   - tests: ADF on the spread, Engle-Granger (`statsmodels.tsa.stattools.coint`), half-life = `−ln 2 / λ` from `Δspread = λ·spread_{t−1} + ε`;
   - trading: enter |z| ≥ 2, exit |z| ≤ 0.5, break stop |z| ≥ 3.5, max holding period;
   - both legs sized from the hedge ratio, costs charged on all four legs per round trip.
2. **`papers/R2_pairs/comparison.md`** containing the cointegration evidence (required by the gate: `coint|ADF|half-life|z-score`), the chosen pairs, the rules, and the walk-forward run.
3. **`papers/R2_pairs/deviations.md`** — universe, formation length, distance metric vs cointegration test, cost assumption, trade frequency.

## Required experiments

- [ ] Selection on the training half only, then trade the second half untouched
- [ ] Selection on the full sample, for comparison — report the Sharpe gap as *measured selection look-ahead*
- [ ] Count of pairs whose spread never reverted in the OOS window (the real risk)
- [ ] Drop the best pair, then the best two
- [ ] Cost sensitivity: four spreads per round trip, swept 0-30 bps

## Definition of done

- [ ] `impl.py`, `comparison.md`, `deviations.md` exist and are committed
- [ ] Test output for every traded pair (p-value and half-life) is in `comparison.md`
- [ ] The selection look-ahead gap is quantified in Sharpe units
- [ ] The break-stop rule is stated numerically and its effect on the trade distribution shown
- [ ] Verdict word and graveyard entries for the pairs that broke permanently

## The two rules that matter most

1. **No full-sample glances during selection.** If you looked at the OOS window while choosing pairs, say so in the deviations log — that is worth more than a clean result.
2. **A break is not a bargain.** `|z| > 3.5` means the relationship is failing; adding to the position there is the single most common way students die on this deliverable.
