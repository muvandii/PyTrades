# D10 — Strategies S04-S06 + bootstrap confidence intervals

## Objective

Extend the factory to breakout, cross-sectional momentum and pairs trading, and attach a confidence interval to every survivor's headline number.

## Teaches

Every backtest number is a sample from a distribution you have not seen. A bootstrap CI does not make a weak result strong; it makes a weak result *honestly weak*, which is the point.

## Build

For each of `S04_donchian`, `S05_momentum`, `S06_pairs`:

1. Spec with thesis, signal, in-sample and out-of-sample runs, cost sweep, tearsheet, verdict - the D08 checklist.
2. Family-specific evidence:
   - **S05 momentum**: the monthly rebalance turnover and the rank stability of holdings between rebalances.
   - **S06 pairs**: the pair selection rule, the training-window-only selection, and the spread's half-life.
3. **Bootstrap**: for every strategy with a positive OOS Sharpe, `reports/bootstrap.md` containing:
   - block-bootstrap Sharpe distribution (2,000 resamples; block length = typical holding period in bars),
   - 5-95% interval, and whether it excludes zero,
   - the same for mean daily return, with the t-statistic and the independent-bet count.
4. **`strategies/index.md`** updated with every new row.

## Definition of done

- [ ] Three new verdicts, each with OOS numbers net of costs
- [ ] `reports/bootstrap.md` covers every positive-Sharpe survivor with a CI and a t-stat
- [ ] S06 documents the pair selection procedure and the training window used
- [ ] At least one strategy's CI includes zero, and its verdict says so instead of claiming an edge
- [ ] Registry updated; graveyard updated for any new deaths
