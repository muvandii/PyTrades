# D08 — Strategies S01-S03

## Objective

Take three signal families through the identical pipeline - spec, signal, backtest, cost sweep, tearsheet, verdict - and produce three artifacts that a stranger could compare side by side.

## Teaches

Signal families fail in family-specific ways, and naming the family predicts the autopsy. Trend systems die of chop and whipsaw costs; short-horizon reversion dies of spreads; both die of being crowded, but their numbers die differently.

## Build

For each of `S01_ma_cross`, `S02_rsi_reversion`, `S03_bollinger`:

1. **Spec** (`config/<id>.json`) written before the code, thesis included, per [spec/strategy-config-schema.md](../../../shared/templates/strategy-config-schema.md).
2. **Signal** (`strategies/<id>/signal.py`) implementing `generate(prices, params)` - no engine imports, no file access, weights as targets.
3. **Runs**: in-sample and out-of-sample names (`NAME=s01_is`, `NAME=s01_oos`), plus a cost sweep (`make costs S=<id>`).
4. **Tearsheet** generated by `engine/tearsheet.py` into `strategies/<id>/tearsheet.md`.
5. **Verdict** (`strategies/<id>/verdict.md`) from `shared/templates/verdict.md`, including:
   - headline numbers net of costs, out-of-sample;
   - cost-sensitivity table with breakeven bps;
   - fragility checks (plateau width, bootstrap CI, largest-trade concentration);
   - the verdict word, the mechanism sentence, and kill criteria;
   - the graveyard line if dead.

## Definition of done

- [ ] Three specs, three signals, three generated tearsheets, three verdicts
- [ ] Every verdict includes a breakeven cost figure and an OOS number
- [ ] At least one verdict names the family-specific failure mode explicitly
- [ ] `reports/sensitivity/<id>.csv` exists for at least one of the three
- [ ] Every strategy appears in `strategies/index.md` (D09)
