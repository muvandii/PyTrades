# D09 — Strategy registry + graveyard v1

## Objective

Turn "I tested some stuff" into a track record: one table listing every strategy with its numbers, its verdict and its evidence, plus a graveyard with at least two properly-autopsied deaths.

## Teaches

A registry is the difference between research and a hobby. It also forces honesty: writing OOS Sharpe, breakeven cost and verdict next to each other in one table makes it impossible to remember a strategy as better than it was.

## Build

1. **`strategies/index.md`** with one row per strategy:

   | id | family | universe | period | IS Sharpe | OOS Sharpe | turnover/yr | breakeven bps | verdict | evidence |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

   Rules: numbers are net of costs; OOS is the pooled walk-forward result where available; the
   `evidence` column links the verdict file. No row without a verdict, no verdict without an artifact.

2. **`graveyard.md`** with at least two entries, each using the taxonomy cause from
   `shared/templates/graveyard-entry.md`, plus the specific number that killed it.

3. A five-line summary at the top of `index.md`: how many tested, how many dead, the most common
   cause of death so far, and the current best survivor with one caveat attached to it.

## Definition of done

- [ ] `strategies/index.md` exists with a row per tested strategy and a linked verdict
- [ ] No row claims a number that is not in a saved artifact
- [ ] `graveyard.md` has 2+ entries with taxonomy causes and the killer number
- [ ] The five-line summary names the most common cause of death
- [ ] `live/config.json` exists (the 30-day paper-trading clock started this week)

## Evidence
