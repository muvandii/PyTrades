# D02 — Measurement lab: first equity curves + expectancy table

## Objective

Produce the two measurement instruments every later week reuses: a correctly compounded equity curve, and a trade-level expectancy table.

## Teaches

An equity curve is the only honest scoreboard, and expectancy is its atom. Win rate is a marketing number; expectancy is the physics. This deliverable exists to make you feel the difference on data you already understand.

## Build

**Part A — equity curves (`reports/measurement-lab.md` section 1-2)**

1. Buy-and-hold SPY: `equity = initial * (1 + returns).cumprod()`. Plot it.
2. Compute total return twice - `(1+r).prod() - 1` and `r.sum()` - and write both numbers down. Explain the gap in one sentence.
3. The recovery exhibit: -50% then +50%. Write the arithmetic longhand.
4. Build three baselines: equal-weight buy-and-hold of your universe, random entries with a 1-month hold (200 runs, show the distribution), and always-flat cash.
