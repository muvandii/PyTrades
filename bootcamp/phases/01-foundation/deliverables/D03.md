# D03 — Viral debunk #1 (timed, 30 minutes)

## Objective

Take one published "profitable strategy" claim from reconstruction to written verdict in a single timed 30-minute session, and write it up with the elapsed time honestly recorded.

## Teaches

A published win rate is a marketing number until you recompute it from prices with execution, costs and sample size applied. The 30-minute budget is the lesson: with a fixed checklist, debunking stops being research and becomes a reflex.

## Build

1. Pick a claim you have not examined. Quote it verbatim and link the source.
2. Fill `templates/debunk.md` top to bottom. The timed part is Steps 2-4; Step 1 (reconstruction) may be done beforehand, but note how long it took.
3. Run the three killers in order - execution, costs, sample - and stop at the first one that kills the claim. Record the measured effect of each you ran.
4. Write the verdict: `debunked` / `survives (partially)` / `inconclusive`, plus the single mechanism that decided it.

Time log (required): `start __:__ · reconstruct __ min · data __ min · measure __ min · break __ min · verdict __ min · total __ min`

## Definition of done

- [ ] `reports/debunks/debunk-01.md` exists and follows the template
- [ ] Claim is quoted verbatim with its source link
- [ ] The elapsed time (total and per step) is recorded, even if it exceeds 30 minutes
- [ ] The execution check states explicitly whether the signal uses the bar it trades
- [ ] The cost check reports a breakeven bps figure (or states why costs are irrelevant)
- [ ] The sample check states the number of independent bets, not rows
- [ ] A verdict with a named mechanism, plus a graveyard line

## Evidence

`reports/debunks/debunk-01.md` · the reconstruction script or notebook · `graveyard.md` entry
